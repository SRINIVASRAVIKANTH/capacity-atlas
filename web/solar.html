      How to read this map

      1. Circles. Each circle is a ZIP code, sized by the solar connected there.
      2. Inner circle. The smaller circle in the middle shows what was connected in the last 12 months. A large inner circle means recent growth.
      3. Details. Click a circle to see that ZIP code's systems, community solar, storage and primary utility.
      4. Search. Type a ZIP code or town to jump to it.
      5. Room to connect. Use "See room to connect here", or the switch at the top of the panel, to see how much room the nearby lines still have.
      6. Growth. Under "Connected each year", press play to watch the circles grow from the first year on record to today. Drag the slider to stop at any year.

      Glossary

      System
      One solar project in NYSERDA's list, from a rooftop array to a solar farm.
      Community solar
      A shared solar farm that homes and businesses subscribe to instead of installing panels themselves.
      Feeder
      One circuit leaving a substation, with all its branches. Projects on the same feeder share its capacity.

      NYSERDA lists projects by ZIP code, not by address, so each circle sits at the center of its ZIP code area. Where the data comes from: Data sources and terms.
