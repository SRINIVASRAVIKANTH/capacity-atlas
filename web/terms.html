Capacity Atlas

Hosting capacity values are estimates that each utility publishes for early screening. They change when the utility updates its study, and they do not replace a utility interconnection study or application. Before you make a decision about a project, confirm the numbers with the utility. Capacity Atlas is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, approved or endorsed by any utility.

04Privacy

Capacity Atlas has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it sets no cookies.

Stays in your browser

Your display settings, whether you have seen the first-visit tip, and the chat with Volta. Downloads and share links are also made in your browser. None of it is sent to the site.

Place search

What you type in the search box goes to Photon by komoot to find matching places.

Questions to Volta

Your question, and the last three questions of the same chat for context, go to the site's server and on to Anthropic's Claude API to be answered. The site does not keep them. Your IP address is held briefly in memory to limit how many questions each visitor can ask, then forgotten.

Hosting and map files

The site runs on Cloudflare and the basemap comes from OpenFreeMap. As with any website, these services see your IP address when your browser asks them for files, and may keep standard technical logs under their own policies.
